Short-form videos from the conversations your niche is already having.

Research Studio is an AI video studio for creators. It finds what a niche community is talking about, drafts a script in the community's own language, renders a vertical video with voiceover, captions and licensed background footage, and posts it to the creator's own TikTok account only after the creator reviews and approves it.

Research

Surfaces recurring questions and debates from niche communities.

Script

Drafts hooks, scripts and captions that fit how the community talks.

Render

Builds a 9:16 video with AI voiceover, timed captions and animated or licensed backgrounds.

Review & post

Nothing is posted until the creator approves that specific video and picks who can see it.

How the TikTok integration works

The creator connects their own TikTok account with Login Kit. Research Studio reads the account's display name so the creator can see which account is connected, and uses the Content Posting API to upload videos the creator has approved. Before each post, the creator sees the video, the caption and the privacy options TikTok allows for their account. The creator can disconnect TikTok at any time from the Accounts page.

Research Studio is a self-hosted tool run by its operator for their own accounts. It is not a public sign-up service.
